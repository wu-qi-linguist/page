Friends outside of Linguistics call me Dominique

I'm a fourth-year PhD student in Linguistics at University of Connecticut. I'm a theoretical linguist working on Syntax, Semantics, Morphology, and the way structures and meanings interact. As a multi-lingual, I also hold interests in the socio-cultural meaning of languages.

Before diving into language structure, my background was about analyzing how stories and narratives are structured by language.
